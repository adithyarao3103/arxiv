Lead Story · hep-th

Scale-separated quasi-dS and the Swampland

Authors’ note: 128 pages, 6 figures, 7 tables

Ideas from the Swampland program (dS conjecture and TCC) suggest that it is impossible to obtain solutions of string theory/M-theory in the weak-coupling regions describing our current universe, with $V>0$, $\|\nabla V\|/V < 2/\sqrt{d-2}$ and $V \ll m^2$ (scale-separated "quasi-dS") where $m$ is the mass of the light tower. We test this expectation in M-theory compactifications and find that even after including the quantum effect of Casimir energy, for hyperbolic and (rigid) Riemann-flat manifolds, de Sitter critical points under EFT control are excluded: the slope of the potential cannot drop below the asymptotic value predicted by the TCC $\|\nabla V\|/V\gtrsim 2/\sqrt{d-2}$ except perhaps in an order-one region in the bulk of moduli space, where EFT control is lost.

Rafael Álvarez-García et al. · arXiv:2611.10116

Stanley Deser 19th March 1931 - 21st April 2023

Stanley Deser was Ancell Professor Emeritus at Brandeis University and Visiting Professor at the California Institute of Technology. He was a world-renowned theoretical physicist whose accomplishments include the Arnowitt-Deser-Misner (ADM) formulation of general relativity, supergravity with Zumino, the dynamics of flat space with Jackiw and 't Hooft and many other contributions to the quantum theory of gravity.

In the 1980s he was instrumental in securing National Science Foundation approval of the LIGO gravitational wave experiment against much opposition.

M. J. Duff and K. S. Stelle · arXiv:2611.11323

Strings, Amplitudes & Supergravity · hep-th

Two-Loop $n$-Point Identical Helicity Amplitude for Gravity Minimally Coupled to a Chiral Fermion

Motivated by recent developments in the two-loop identical-helicity sector of gauge theory when the one-loop self-dual integrability anomaly of Bardeen and Cangemi, or the equivalent twistor-space version of Costello and Paquette, vanishes, we compute the two-loop four-point identical-helicity amplitude in gravity coupled to a single chiral fermion, for which the corresponding self-dual anomaly also vanishes. We show that the infrared-subtracted amplitude is purely rational and proportional to the identical-helicity tree amplitude of pure gravity with a single insertion of the operator $R^3$.

Zvi Bern et al. · arXiv:2611.12096

Gravity & Cosmology · hep-th

Proof of the QNEC, without assumptions and under a weight

We complete a proof of the quantum null energy condition (QNEC) in the mathematical framework of Half-Sided Translations and Modular Inclusions (HST/HSMI) removing an assumption that applied to previous proofs. Here we establish a theorem, which when translated to a physical setting implies that the relative entropy comparing the state of fields outside a stationary black hole with a Hartle-Hawking (HH) state is always a convex function of the horizon cut.

Thomas Faulkner · arXiv:2611.11920

Proof of the Rényi QNEC

We give a general proof of the Rényi QNEC formulated using the Araki-Masuda weighted $L_p$ norms for all $p > 2$. This generalizes the algebraic proof of the QNEC applicable at $p=2$.

Our approach utilizes a weighted version of a conditional $L_p$ norm which extrapolates the quantum expansion/ant-quantity.

We investigate cubic curvature corrections to four-dimensional Taub-NUT-AdS black holes and their thermodynamics. We construct the complete analytic solution to the first order in the higher-derivative couplings, and derive the corrected Hawking temperature and Wald entropy.
