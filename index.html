Lead Story · hep-th

Elliptic master integrals and the one-loop cosmological wavefunction

Authors’ note: 46 pages, 5 figures, ancillary files included

Loop corrections to the cosmological wavefunction involve similar integrals to those required for scattering amplitudes. For the one-loop triangle of a conformally coupled scalar, the master integrals are elliptic, and so the triangle wavefunction coefficient was expected to be elliptic as well.

Using a bootstrap approach, we compute this triangle coefficient exactly and show that it is not: the elliptic periods cancel, and the flat-space wavefunction coefficient is a combination of dilogarithms. The cancellation occurs because the residue of the loop integrand on the elliptic sector is a flat-space amplitude, rational in the loop momentum.

The one-loop box, by contrast, is not polylogarithmic, even with a single external leg at each site. Its threshold discontinuity involves elliptic integrals, which we also give in closed form.

Amara McCune and Matthew D. Schwartz · arXiv:2610.38725

Strings, Amplitudes & Supergravity · hep-th

Bounds on the Diagonal Degree of the Biadjoint Scalar Amplitude Matrix

Biadjoint scalar theory partial amplitudes, $m_n(α,β)$, define an $(n-1)!/2\times (n-1)!/2$ matrix, $M_n$, with rows and columns labeled by orderings. The CHY construction of $M_n$ implies that its rank is $(n-3)!$.

Invertible submatrices of maximal rank enter in the KLT construction relating graviton amplitudes to gluon amplitudes. Motivated by finding the KLT formula with the smallest number of terms, one of the authors posed the question of whether there is a critical $n_c$ such that for all $n>n_c$ there exists a diagonal submatrix of $M_n$ of rank $(n-3)!$.

Freddy Cachazo and Rajbir Longia · arXiv:2610.40355

Strings, Amplitudes & Supergravity · hep-th

Superstring Field Theory with Double Background Independence

A recently constructed superstring field theory has two decoupled sectors, the physical superstring and a shadow sector with a second copy of the superstring. These are both expanded around the same background conformal field theory and the theory is independent of marginal deformations of this common background.

A form of the action is presented that is symmetric between the two sectors. A generalisation of this theory is given in which the two sectors can be expanded around two different backgrounds, and the theory is independent under separate marginal deformations of the two background conformal field theories.

Chris Hull · arXiv:2610.39594

Holography & Gauge/Gravity · hep-th

Carrollian CFTs and the Light Front

Carrollian symmetries play an important role in building holographic duals to asymptotically flat spacetimes (AFS). This construction hinges on the isomorphism between the (asymptotic) isometries of AFS and the conformal Carroll algebra.

Carroll symmetries also naturally arise on the lightfront, as co-dimension one subalgebras in the Poincare algebra written in lightcone coordinates. In this paper, building on the isomorphism between four dimensional (4d) Poincare and 3d conformal Carroll algebras, we make a connection between physics on the lightfront and Carrollian CFTs.

Arjun Bagchi et al. · arXiv:2610.39450

$\mathcal{N}=4$ Super Yang-Mills Flux Tube in AdS

We study a supersymmetric Wilson line on the boundary of $\mathcal{N}=4$ super Yang-Mills theory in AdS$_4$, or equivalently on a half-space, as a model for exploring the connection between holographic strings and confining flux tubes. The boundary breaks four-dimensional conformal symmetry and is expected to remove integrability, bringing this system closer to a confining string while preserving enough supersymmetry to constrain its dynamics.
