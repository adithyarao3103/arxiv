Monopole-Fermion Scattering from Twisted Conformal Boundary States

Authors’ note: 42 pages, 8 figures

We derive monopole-fermion scattering correlators from conformal boundary states in twisted sectors. The key ingredient is that symmetry-preserving boundary conditions contain sectors twisted by topological symmetry lines.

Their overlaps determine correlators of operators attached to these lines, including the outgoing excitations required by charge conservation in the Callan-Rubakov effect. We construct the twisted boundary states of $N$ free Dirac fermions with $U(1)^N$-preserving boundary conditions, extending the familiar description in terms of charge lattices to shifted lattices describing twisted sectors.

Using cluster decomposition and open-closed duality, we determine their coefficients and fix their normalization relative to the untwisted boundary state.

Clay Córdova et al. · arXiv:2610.40779

On Boundary State Screening via Solitons

We present a coarse classification scheme for the boundary data of massive QFTs based on transitions of boundary states via soliton / kink profiles. Starting from the lattice of boundary states, we introduce the boundary screening group $\mathbb{B}$: the equivalence classes of boundary states which cannot be connected by such transitions.

In string theory backgrounds which geometrically engineer an interacting QFT in the target space, $\mathbb{B}$ computes the worldsheet analog of the target space defect group $\mathbb{D}$.

Vivek Chakrabhavi et al. · arXiv:2610.40777

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Giant Gravitons in AdS$_5\times$SE$_5$ and the Superconformal Index of Toric Quiver Gauge Theories

We derive giant-graviton contributions to superconformal indices by quantizing probe D3-branes in type IIB backgrounds $\mathrm{AdS}_{5}\times SE_{5}$. For $SE_{5}=T^{1,1}$ and $Y^{p,q}$, we determine the scalar, vector, and fermionic fluctuations of maximal giant gravitons and extract their BPS spectra.

The resulting single-letter indices agree with those predicted by the giant-graviton expansions of the dual toric quiver gauge theories.

Evan Deddo et al. · arXiv:2610.40773

Holography & Gauge/Gravity · hep-th

Umbilic Observatories

We study gravitational dynamics for the simplest example of an umbilic boundary in four-dimensional general relativity --- a three-dimensional de Sitter hypersurface embedded in a four-dimensional Minkowski world. Generally speaking, an umbilic surface has the property that its induced metric is proportional to its extrinsic curvature.

We consider the case of both conformal boundary conditions, as well as Dirichlet boundary conditions imposed at the timelike hypersurface.

Dionysios Anninos et al. · arXiv:2610.40783

We propose a remote detectability criterion for two-dimensional conformal field theories, which asserts that any local operator outside of the product of the left- and right-moving Virasoro algebras can be detected by some topological line defect. Proof of the entropic $c$-theorem

We prove an entropic $c$-theorem in two dimensions by constructing a $c$-function from the mutual information between spacelike separated wedges, defined through the relative entropy. The proof follows Casini and Huerta's, with strong subadditivity of entanglement entropy replaced by increasing differences of the mutual information, which we prove directly in the continuum from the split property and standard properties of relative entropy.

Jonah Kudler-Flam · arXiv:2610.40874

Gravity & Cosmology

Quantum Cosmology at the Cusp

In top-down quantum gravity constructions, saxionic directions typically lead to cusps in moduli space, where the scalar potential and the axion decay constant asymptotically behave as $V\sim e^{-αφ}$ and $f\sim e^{-γφ}$ in reduced Planck units. Such cusps admit axion-supported scaling cosmologies that expand faster than the corresponding solutions with the axion held fixed, for the same scalar potential.

Bootstrapping a Higher-Derivative Cosmological Collider: Borel-Laplace Resummation and Stokes Phenomena

We develop a higher-derivative cosmological bootstrap for massive spectators with quartic dispersion, $ω^2\propto k^4$, revealing a new boundary-value problem for inflationary correlators. The exchange equation becomes fourth order, and the collapsed limit becomes an irregular singular point, where collider and WKB branches coexist and exhibit Stokes phenomena.

Separation of variables for massive spin-2 fields on the Kerr black hole

Massive spin-2 fields on the Kerr black hole generally obey coupled partial differential equations. We show that these equations separate through two commuting angular operators acting on five components of the field, its curl and its linearised Weyl tensor.

A joint angular eigenfunction fixes the relative profiles of the five polarisations and leaves one second-order radial equation.
