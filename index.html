Lead Story · hep-th

5d SCFTs and Intersection Theory of Generalized Toric Threefolds

Five-dimensional superconformal field theories are intrinsically strongly coupled fixed points that can be realized as M-theory on local Calabi-Yau threefolds with canonical singularities. The low-energy effective theory on their extended Coulomb branch is governed by a cubic prepotential, determined by the triple intersection numbers of divisors in the resolved geometry.

For toric threefolds, dual to 5-brane webs, these numbers are computed combinatorially from the toric polygon. However, no such description has been available for generalized toric polygons (GTPs), dual to 5-brane webs with 7-branes, since the corresponding generalized toric Calabi-Yau threefolds are generally non-toric.

We show that the 7-brane data are encoded by additional lines in the GTP, which correspond to non-toric, non-compact divisors that we call exuberant divisors.

Hülya Argüz et al. · arXiv:2611.00366

The Virasoro Bootstrap: from the $c\sim1$ desert to coupled Potts

We develop a numerical conformal bootstrap for two-dimensional CFTs with $c>1$ based on Virasoro conformal blocks, computed with Zamolodchikov's recursion and optimal polynomial interpolation. As a first test we isolate an island around three decoupled Ising models.

We then consider three 3-state Potts models coupled through their energy operators, a candidate irrational CFT. Mixed correlators isolate the decoupled theory in an island, and different gap assumptions, motivated by perturbation theory, yield a peninsula that contains the coupled fixed point but not the decoupled one.

Stefanos R. Kousvos et al. · arXiv:2611.03565

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

I want to Break Free: The Positive Side of Symmetry Breaking

We study the role of internal field-space symmetries in Effective Field Theories constrained by positivity bounds. For theories with $N$ real scalar fields, we classify derivative operators according to the internal symmetries they preserve, organising them into hierarchies based on the maximal symmetry $O(N)$ and its subgroups.

We find that the Wilson coefficients of operators that depart from an enhanced symmetry are bounded in terms of those that respect it.

Anubha Bal and Claudia de Rham · arXiv:2611.03510

Holography & Gauge/Gravity · hep-th

Summing over Seifert Manifolds in Narain Ensemble Averages

The ensemble average of Narain conformal field theories over their moduli space is a real-analytic Eisenstein series, whose Poincaré expansion can be interpreted as a sum over lens spaces weighted by the partition functions of Abelian Chern-Simons theory. In this paper we extend this correspondence from lens spaces to Seifert manifolds, which gives an example of a restricted sum over topologies of the kind that arises in three-dimensional quantum gravity.

Kei Kaneda and Masahito Yamazaki · arXiv:2611.02515

The pinning-field line defect at finite temperature

We study the pinning-field line defect in the critical $\mathrm{O}(N)$ model at finite temperature, describing a static symmetry-breaking impurity at a quantum critical point. Using the operator product expansion (OPE), analyticity, and thermal periodicity, we construct a manifestly periodic expansion relating heavy-operator asymptotics to low-lying thermal OPE data.

A local action for Kodaira--Spencer gravity

A local action is given for Kodaira--Spencer gravity by solving a constraint on the complex structure deformation of a Calabi--Yau threefold in terms of a $(1,1)$ form as the new gravity field. The gauge algebra becomes a genuine $L_{\infty}$ algebra based on a Courant algebroid, while the gravity field and its linearized gauge transformations coincide with those of double field theory.

Symmetry breaking from conformality loss on holographic Rényi defects

The Rényi entropies of a ball are computed by a conformal defect, the Rényi defect, dual in holography to a hyperbolic black hole. At an index $n_*$ its charged operator merges with its alternate-quantization partner, and the defect loses conformality.

Beyond $n_*$ it flows with no source to a conformal defect that breaks the U(1) symmetry; below $n_*$ that defect needs a symmetry-breaking source, so at large $N$, conformality loss makes the breaking spontaneous.

Siqing Yu · arXiv:2611.03413

Higher Groupoids of Gauge Potentials in Higher Gauge Theories admitting Weights

We construct an explicit small model for the $\infty$-groupoid of null-concordances of higher gauge curvatures by algorithmically trivializing their nonlinear Bianchi identities. More precisely, for a finite-type Sullivan minimal Bianchi algebra equipped with positive integer weights, we organize weighted potentials and higher gauge parameters at each fixed curvature into an $\infty$-groupoid whose gauge transformations are linear in the parameters.
