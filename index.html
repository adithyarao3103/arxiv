Equivariant iterated Eisenstein integrals for congruence subgroups of level 2

Authors’ note: 92+36 pages

The modular properties of holomorphic Eisenstein series for $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ and its congruence subgroups do not carry over to their primitives. Instead, the transformation of iterated Eisenstein integrals under congruence subgroups of level $N$ introduces inhomogeneous terms comprising (among others) cyclotomic multiple zeta values of level $N$.

We combine iterated Eisenstein integrals for the congruence subgroups of level $N=2$ of arbitrary iteration depth with their complex conjugates and (single-valued) level-2 cyclotomic multiple zeta values such that they define non-holomorphic modular forms. Our construction of these so-called equivariant iterated Eisenstein integrals for $Γ(2)$ and $Γ_0(2)$ proceeds at the level of generating series that take values in an infinite-dimensional (pro-unipotent) Lie group and closely follow Brown's analogous construction in the $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ case.

Emiel Claasen et al. · arXiv:2611.26597

Second-order formulation of chiral fermions with a local boundary condition

Chiral fermions in $d$ dimensions arise naturally as boundary modes of a massive fermion in $d+1$ dimensions subject to a local boundary condition. When anomalies are absent, this construction can be used to define an intrinsically $d$-dimensional theory of chiral fermions.

However, defining the path integral using the first-order Dirac operator with this boundary condition is technically inconvenient, even in the continuum.

Kazuya Yonekura · arXiv:2611.25749

Strings, Amplitudes & Supergravity · hep-th

From Integrability to Chaos in Tensionless AdS3 String Theory

We study quantum-gravitational corrections to the planar integrable structure of the symmetric product orbifold $\text{Sym}^N(\mathbb{T}^4)$ that is dual to tensionless string theory on AdS$_3\times$S$^3\times \mathbb{T}^4$. We consider the deformation that switches on R-R flux in the bulk, and analyze the associated level spacing statistics.

The leading non-planar corrections lift the planar degeneracies and produce signs of random-matrix behavior.

Matthias R. Gaberdiel et al. · arXiv:2611.25576

Cardinal RG=GR: Phase-Space Cardinality and the Cosmological Constant and Hierarchy Problems

The effective field theory approach constructs the low-energy theory by integrating out fluctuations above an ultraviolet scale which raises the cosmological constant and hierarchy problems. We investigate an alternative prescription in which the quantum theory is restricted to a finite accessible region of phase space, characterized by a dimensionless cardinality $\mathcal N$, rather than by independently prescribed spacetime and momentum scales.

Dynamical Temperley-Lieb algebras from Quiver CFTs

The one-loop dilatation operator of a superconformal planar gauge theory can be written as a sum of local generators. The algebra these generators satisfy is a powerful organising principle for the spectrum.

For SU(2)-like bosonic subsectors we show that this algebra can be read off directly from Feynman diagrams, which we identify as a natural graphical language for it.

Upper Bounds on the Radion Mass in a Warped Extra Dimension

We study absolute upper bounds on the mass ratio $R_{11}=m_{r_1}/m_{h_1}$ between the radion ($r_1$) and graviton ($h_1$) lightest modes in five dimensional warped models. We consider warped models with an admissible singular behavior in the infrared: i.e.~models with power-like ($\sim \barφ^{2n}$) and with exponential ($\sim e^{2ν\barφ}$) bulk potentials, where $\barφ$ is the stabilizing bulk field.

Soft Factorisation of WQFT and the Spinning 4PM Magnusian

We derive the soft divergences of $\hat{N}=-i\log\hat{S}$ in the Worldline Quantum Field Theory (WQFT) formalism, focusing on poles due to single massive-massive (Coulomb) and massive-massless (tail) graviton exchanges. UV Bounds on Inflationary Parity Violation

A question of fundamental interest is whether parity is violated on large scales in the Universe. This possibility has been motivated by recent analyses of the four-point matter correlation function.

It has also been suggested that detectable parity violation could be generated during inflation by the dynamical Chern-Simons operator, a leading effective field theory correction to general relativity.

Equilibrium relativistic spin magnetohydrodynamics from chiral kinetic theory

We develop a microscopic and thermodynamically consistent framework for relativistic equilibrium spin magnetohydrodynamics of massless chiral fermions, directly from first-order chiral kinetic theory. Starting from the covariant Wigner function, we recast the chiral kinetic equation as a pure streaming equation on a magnetically shifted mass shell, incorporating both the magnetic-moment energy and the Berry-curvature phase-space measure.

Entropy Products and the Extremal Limit That Never Comes

Two regular black hole horizons need not have an entropy product fixed by the charges and couplings. In Einstein--Maxwell--scalar theory, we construct asymptotically flat branches with products above or below the squared extremal entropy.

Regularity selects their horizon scalar values, and different branches can share the charges, couplings, and asymptotic scalar.

Inhomogeneous Cutoff Regularization of Loops in Cosmological Correlators

We revisit the use of cutoff regularization in computing loop corrections to cosmological correlators. We argue that a cutoff corresponding to a fixed physical scale must be defined with respect to the local background geometry.

In the presence of superhorizon perturbations, this is naturally implemented within the separate universe picture, where different local patches are regulated at the same physical scale.

Stringy Corrections from Gravity: Predictions for BFSS and ABJM

Despite enormous progress in string theory, the leading stringy corrections to many holographic observables remain unknown. String/M-theory dualities nonetheless allow them to be determined from gravity alone.

From the winding modes of one-loop 11d supergravity on a circle, we derive the leading tree-level stringy terms in the type IIA effective action for the metric, dilaton, and Ramond--Ramond one-form, in closed form as a finite supertrace over Lorentz generators.

Notes on non-equilibrium thermodynamics of Robinson-Trautman spacetimes

Robinson-Trautman spacetimes are determined by a parabolic heat-type evolution equation of fourth order for a scalar field on future null infinity. This equation may be re-written as an energy balance for an asymptotically time-like observer.

In turn, it becomes a first law in the context of non-equilibrium thermodynamics if the non-linear memory is interpreted as heat.
