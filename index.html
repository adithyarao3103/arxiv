Lead Story · hep-th

Landau singularity of the K3 maximal cut in black-hole scattering at fifth post-Minkowskian order

Authors’ note: 54 pages, 1 figure

Modeling the gravitational waves from merging black holes requires precise calculations of the two-body problem. The post-Minkowskian expansion of black-hole scattering, in powers of Newton's constant, now stands at fifth order, four loops.

At this order one part of the deflection diverges when the black holes pass at about 94 percent of the speed of light. The divergence is known to come from periods of a K3 surface, which has a node at this speed.

We show that the node lies on the integration contour of the K3 maximal cut, which makes this speed a Landau singularity of the cut. Requiring the divergence to cancel, without introducing new infinities, has been shown to fix the memory contribution, named for the permanent displacement that the waves leave in test masses, up to one constant.

Matthew D. Schwartz · arXiv:2610.36128

On 2-loop AdS$_2$ superstring partition function in AdS$_5 \times S^5$ and AdS$_4\times CP^3$

We revisit the 2-loop computation of the partition function of the GS superstring expanded near the AdS$_2$ surface in AdS$_5\times S^5$ and AdS$_4\times {\rm CP}^3$, dual to $\frac{1}{2}$ BPS circular Wilson loop in ${\cal N}=4$ SYM and ABJM theories. In arXiv:2601.08809 it was done in AdS$_5\times S^5$ using dimensional regularization and a UV pole was found.

Since dimensional regularization is problematic for the GS string, here we use a heat-kernel cutoff instead.

Arkady A. Tseytlin · arXiv:2610.37096

The Disordered Universe 2025: Open Effective Field Theories

Effective field theories (EFTs) offer a powerful method to unify diverse models under a small set of control parameters, allowing systematic expansions around well-established theories. While these techniques were traditionally developed for particle physics experiments with clean, isolated initial states, realistic macroscopic environments are often noisy and dissipative.

Recognising the limitations of traditional EFTs has recently sparked intense progress at the interface of high-energy physics and condensed matter.

Local Measures in Odd de Sitter

We show that massless theories in odd-dimensional de Sitter space have very simple measures---the modulus squared of the late-time wavefunctional---at tree level. They are local functionals; in particular, all wavefunction coefficients vanish at separated positions.
