Cosmic Censorship in the Landscape of SUSY-Broken AdS Vacua

Authors’ note: 72 pages, 2 tables, 11 figures

We investigate signatures of weak cosmic censorship in non-supersymmetric vacua of both IIA and IIB string theory. In particular, we examine the Penrose Inequality, which can be proven holographically using Ryu-Takayanagi, and can be independently proven in SUSY AdS vacua for spherically symmetric initial data.

We prove that any EFT of scalars coupled to gravity in AdS, including in particular those of string compactifications, satisfies both the Penrose Inequality and a positive mass theorem if a function that we call a "weak fake superpotential" exists. We demonstrate that this condition is satisfied globally for the DGKT skew-whiffed vacua in IIA.

Since there is no known nonperturbative instability in this DGKT vacuum, this provides evidence that the IIA SUSY-broken AdS vacua may be absolutely stable.

Netta Engelhardt and Richard Nally · arXiv:2611.21722

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Localization identities for spectral determinants: two-dimensional QCD and Ising field theory

Spectral determinants encode the meson spectra of planar two-dimensional QCD and of Ising field theory in the two-particle Bethe-Salpeter approximation. We derive localization identities for these determinants which express their logarithmic derivatives with respect to the spectral parameter in terms of Baxter $Q-$functions evaluated at special points, proving previously conjectured relations.

Our derivation uses finite-rank commutation relations and virial identities of the underlying integral spectral operators.

Bercel Boldis et al. · arXiv:2611.21724

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Structure Constants of Planar N=4 SYM at Any Coupling

We recast a recent conjecture for structure constants in planar N=4 supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory in a separation-of-variables-inspired form suitable for non-perturbative analysis. All state-dependent information enters through the quantum spectral curve.

As a showcase, we compute at finite coupling the three-point functions of two half-BPS operators and the spin-two member of the Konishi multiplet. Our results span weak to strong coupling, reproduce the known perturbative and string-theory limits, and are consistent with conformal-bootstrap bounds.

Simon Ekhammar et al. · arXiv:2611.21843

Strings, Amplitudes & Supergravity · hep-th

An infrared-safe $S$-matrix for $\mathcal N=4$ super Yang-Mills

Scattering amplitudes of massless gauge theories are not observables: they are infrared divergent, and the standard regulators are tied either to perturbation theory or the planar limit. We study $\mathcal N=4$ super Yang-Mills theory on rigid AdS$_4$ with Dirichlet boundary conditions, where the role of the amplitude is played by correlation functions of boundary operators.

These are finite, and they are defined at any coupling and for any gauge group.

Maria Nocchi and Leonardo Rastelli · arXiv:2611.21713

Field Theory & Symmetry

CFTs with Large Virasoro Primary Gaps

We construct the first explicit examples of compact unitary bosonic full conformal field theories (CFTs) in two dimensions with a unique vacuum and an all-spin Virasoro-primary gap greater than two. Our constructions use asymmetric orbifolds of lattice CFTs built from even unimodular lattices of sufficiently large minimum norm, ensuring that no charged state lies below the desired gap.

Ziming Ji and Jiahua Tian · arXiv:2611.23231

Gravity/Thermodynamics Correspondence for Generic Dynamical Spacetimes

Working within covariant phase space formalism, we establish \textit{gravity-thermodynamics correspondence} for generic dynamical spacetimes. We derive local first and second laws of thermodynamics on general null boundaries, for generic non-stationary solutions to Einstein gravity {with minimally coupled matter} for physical observers that we carefully specify.

Modular conjugations for thermal states and second worlds

The modular conjugation $J$ maps an algebra of observables onto its commutant. For the vacuum on a Rindler wedge it is a spacetime reflection, but for thermal states in two-dimensional CFTs the known expressions involve chiral fields at the complex points $x\pm iβ/2$, interpreted as belonging to a ``second world''.

All-Genus 2D $\mathcal{N}=1$ Superconformal Blocks

We develop a numerical algorithm for two dimensional $\mathcal{N}=1$ superconformal blocks at arbitrary genera, in arbitrary plumbing channels, and for arbitrary spin structures. The algorithm utilizes two commuting copies of Virasoro subalgebras of the direct sum of the $\mathcal{N}=1$ superconformal algebra with an auxiliary free fermion algebra, which allows us to reduce the $\mathcal{N}=1$ superconformal blocks to Virasoro conformal blocks.

Beyond the point lens: radial structure and the horizon in wave-optics gravitational lensing

Gravitational-wave lensing in the wave-optics regime is sensitive to the structure of the lens beyond its total mass: to its exterior radial profile and, as we show, to the boundary condition at its centre. We develop an analytic framework to compute these effects for a scalar wave, as corrections to the wave lensed by a Newtonian point mass.

Convergence of the quasinormal-mode expansion of the Kerr Green function

Black-hole ringdown is usually modeled as a linear superposition of quasinormal modes, and fits to gravitational-wave observations increasingly include high overtones. However, the overtone sum does not necessarily converge: overtones are more strongly damped and thus decay faster in time, but their amplitudes in the Green function (the signal at an observer produced by an impulse at a source point) grow, so the mode description may break down before a finite time.

Davide Ciattaglia et al. · arXiv:2611.23110
