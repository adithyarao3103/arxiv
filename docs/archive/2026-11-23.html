The Kerr Metric in Canonical Harmonic Coordinates to All Orders

Authors’ note: 36 pages. Codes available at Github https://github.com/pierrevanhove/kerr-metric-harmonic and Zenodo https://zenodo.org/records/24397114

We give in closed form the transformation from Boyer--Lindquist coordinates to the canonical harmonic coordinates of the Kerr metric, to all orders in Newton's constant and in the spin. This is the canonical harmonic chart selected by the MPM prescription and by the harmonic-gauge Einstein recursion sourced by the Kerr multipoles, with the residual homogeneous freedom fixed by the boundary conditions.

The chart is the real, time-reversal-covariant combination of the two radial harmonic functions underlying the Cook--Scheel coordinates, which differ from it by a definite residual harmonic transformation. The proof rests on a classification of the residual harmonic freedom.

Rotational covariance, parity, time reversal and analyticity in the spin allow a decaying homogeneous solution beyond the linear field only in the spatial components at third post-Minkowskian order, with angular degree $\ell=p+2$ at spin order $a^p$.

Amina Sadu and Pierre Vanhove · arXiv:2611.27573

Strings, Amplitudes & Supergravity · hep-th

Gauging background isometries of the ILST null string

We present a general gauging of a subalgebra, $\mathfrak h$, of the isometries $\mathfrak g$ of the background geometry for the ILST string (e-Print: hep-th/9307108 [hep-th]). We discuss which of the isometries in $\mathfrak g$ survive the gauging as symmetries.

The gauged symmetry does not in general involve transformations of the vector density V. There is thus a corresponding contraction of the algebra of constraints as compared to the flat background and it no longer represent the full Carroll-Weyl algebra.

Ulf Lindström · arXiv:2611.27437

Holography & Gauge/Gravity · hep-th

BMS Charges from Null Hypersurfaces

We show how the gravitational dynamics, phase space and Noether charges of the BMS group at future null infinity $\mathscr{I}^+$ can be derived as limits of their analogues on null hypersurfaces at finite distance. The Bondi mass-loss and angular-momentum evolution equations arise as limits of the Raychaudhuri and Damour constraints.

The Ashtekar--Streubel (aka Wald--Zoupas) symplectic potential arises as a specific member of a two-parameter family of null-boundary symplectic potentials, corresponding to the Dirichlet (aka canonical) polarization, while the other members differ by field-space exact terms, leading to the same radiative pre-symplectic structure but potentially different BMS fluxes.

Martina Adamo et al. · arXiv:2611.27488

Strings, Amplitudes & Supergravity · hep-th

String breaking in a (1+1)-dimensional $e^+e^-$ collider

In this paper, we develop a framework for studying solitons in $(1+1)$-dimensional gauge theories using tensor networks, and we apply it to simulate $e^+e^-$ collisions in the massive Schwinger model. We work at $θ= π$ and at a bare fermion mass exceeding the critical value; in this regime, there are two degenerate vacua, and the $e^+$ and $e^-$ particles are realized as soliton states interpolating between them.

We carefully explain how to find and characterize such soliton states using infinite tensor network methods, and then exhibit real-time simulations of their collisions.

Field Theory & Symmetry

Neural variational Skyrmions as a topological benchmark: emergent symmetry, precision energies and KAN--MLP comparison

We propose the three-dimensional Skyrme model as a topological benchmark for neural variational methods and construct fully three-dimensional Skyrmions with baryon number $B=1,\ldots,4$ without imposing a symmetry ansatz, with high precision. For $B=2,3,4$, the relaxation of these Skyrmions starts from $B$ unit-charge lumps with random positions and orientations, which carry no symmetry information.

Pairing and modular: Generalized electrons and quasiholes inside the $\boxtimes$ of topological orders

We study the relationship between the pair creation mechanism in physics and the pairing ($\boxtimes_{\mathbf{A}_{\text{ub}}}$) in mathematics, focusing on the modular and algebraic properties. We provide a way to couple two topologically ordered systems, $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{A'}$ sharing the common (non-group-like) structure $\mathbf{A}_{\text{ub}}=\overline{\mathbf{A'}_{\text{ub}}}$, through the nonabelian anyon analog of the pair creation mechanism denoted as $\mathbf{A}_{\text{ub},\boxtimes}=\mathbf{A}_{\text{ub}}\boxtimes \mathbf{A'}_{\text{ub}}$.

A covariant analysis of massless Dirac fields in LRS spacetimes

We investigate the massless Dirac field in locally rotationally symmetric (LRS) spacetimes using a covariant spinorial-fluid representation, with the spinor current and spin direction aligned with the LRS frame. When the current has both vorticity and sheet twist, characterized by a constant ratio $λ$, the LRS integrability condition relates evolution and propagation derivatives, reducing the field equations to an ordinary differential system that can be integrated exactly.
