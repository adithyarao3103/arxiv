Lead Story · hep-th

Onsager Algebras and Lattice Fermions

Authors’ note: 36 pages + appendices

We study the exact Onsager algebra of free lattice fermions in staggered, Wilson, and overlap incarnations, working in both Hamiltonian and Euclidean formulations in four spacetime dimensions. In each case, we show that the exact lattice algebra emanates the full chiral symmetry of the low-energy theory: if there are $N$ massless Dirac fermions in the continuum, the Onsager algebra reduces to a $U(2N)$ chiral symmetry.

The details of which generators are quantised or ultra-local differ between formulations. There is also a trade-off between the locality of generators and the maximum compact subgroup of the Onsager algebra.

Ioanna Kourkoulou and David Tong · arXiv:2610.31653

Holography & Gauge/Gravity · hep-th

Quantum gravity on the bulk cone

Bulk-cone singularities in holographic correlators encode propagation along bulk null geodesics. The prototypical example is an AdS black hole, where incoming null geodesics can wind around the photon sphere and return to the boundary.

These singularities appear in real time in the thermal two-point function at finite volume. We show that conformal field theories dual to gravitational theories at finite $G_N$ cannot have such singularities.

Ahmed Almheiri et al. · arXiv:2610.31610

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Surface Defects in the $\mathcal{N}=2$ Lens Space Index and Elliptic Integrability

We determine the surface-defect difference operators of the 4d $\mathcal{N}=2$ lens space index for class $\mathcal{S}$ theories of type $A_{N-1}$ on $(\mathbb{S}^3/\mathbb{Z}_r)\times\mathbb{S}^1$. For generic fugacities $(p,q,t)$ and arbitrary rank $N$, orbifold order $r$ and discrete holonomies, their coefficients are finite products of theta functions with nomes $p^r$ and $q^r$.

After an explicit gauge transformation, Gaussian in the holonomies, each operator factorizes into two elliptic Ruijsenaars--Schneider-type operators with step $pq$, one with nome $p^r$ and one with nome $q^r$, acting on variables glued along a lattice.

Masahito Yamazaki · arXiv:2610.30849

Gravity & Cosmology · hep-th

Quasinormal modes, complex null geodesics and singularity resolution in regular AdS black holes

The black hole singularity is hidden behind its horizon, and it is difficult to determine whether it is resolved by quantum gravity or stringy effects. For an AdS-Schwarzschild black hole, it is known that the two-point function of the dual conformal field theory has a singularity associated with a null geodesic that bounces off the black hole singularity.

In this paper, we investigate how the resolution of the black hole singularity is encoded in the dual conformal field theory by considering regular AdS black holes as regularized geometries.

Heng-Yu Chen et al. · arXiv:2610.30915

Five-loop static two-body potential in massless scalar-tensor gravity

We compute the static contribution to the conservative two-body potential in massless scalar-tensor theories through fifth post-Newtonian (5PN) order, with the strong-field scalar responses of the compact objects encoded in scalar-dependent masses. Within post-Newtonian effective field theory (EFT), evaluating the 1PN-5PN static terms requires one- through five-loop massless two-point Feynman integrals.

Amplified EFT corrections near exceptional QNMs

We show that the ordinary expansion of EFT corrections to individual QNM frequencies breaks down at an exceptional QNM, and that these corrections become enhanced for near-exceptional QNMs. We give a brief argument for why this should generally happen, before numerically demonstrating this behaviour by calculating the $α'^3$ corrections to QNMs of equal-spin Myers--Perry-AdS$_5$ black holes.

The Spectral Edge at Large Central Charge and Black Hole Statistics

We introduce a new approach to high-energy spectra in AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$, addressing the open challenge of incorporating spectral statistics into the modular bootstrap. Using the analytic theory of $L$-functions attached to two-dimensional CFTs, we leverage modular constraints among high-energy states to derive a sum rule relating the spectral edge to bulk spectral fluctuations.

Beyond the Fock Vacuum in Finite-$N$ Bilocal Algebras of Vector Models

We discuss the finite-$N$ Hilbert space of bilocal operators that underlies bilocal holography of vector models, beyond the sector built on the Fock vacuum. If the microscopic oscillator Fock space carries a multiplicity-free dual action of a compact color group $G$ and a bilocal algebra, Howe duality implies that projection to the derived subgroup $[G,G]$ selects precisely the characters of the abelianization $G^{\rm ab}=G/[G,G]$ that occur in the Fock space.

Krylov Complexity and Entropy of Composite Quantum Systems

We investigate the behaviour of Krylov complexity as the number of degrees of freedom of a quantum system increases. Specifically, we show that for tensor products of commuting subsystems, the leading excess complexity is controlled by a weighted variance of the subsystem Lanczos coefficients.

Data analysis of millions of Type IIB flux vacua

We study the vacuum structure of flux compactifications of type IIB string theory on the degree 18 hypersurface in $\mathbb{CP}^4_{1,1,1,6,9}$ with two effective complex structure moduli. We scan $30{,}770{,}178$ flux choices compatible with the tadpole bound, and use a gradient-based constrained minimization based on the SLSQP algorithm to identify candidates for local minima of the scalar potential, yielding a dataset of millions of candidate flux vacua.

Ricci-flat Seeds, Trombone Rescaling and Magnetizing Kerr Black Holes

We present a new family of Ricci-flat rotating metrics, which we refer to as Solution II, obtained by demagnetizing the genuine Kerr-Bertotti-Robinson (G-KBR) black hole. Together with Solution I, which was previously obtained by demagnetizing the Kerr-Bertotti-Robinson (KBR) black hole, this gives two families of Ricci-flat deformations of the Kerr black hole, each labeled by $B$.

This thesis investigates the perturbative dynamics and phenomenology of compact objects in two approaches to quantum gravity: quadratic gravity and asymptotic safety. Linear perturbations provide a tool to probe the physical properties of spacetimes and their potential observational signatures, including quasinormal modes, black hole shadows, and Hawking radiation.

Gravitational waves from highly charged binary black holes in Einstein-Maxwell-dilaton theory

We perform fully nonlinear numerical-relativity simulations of electrically charged binary black hole mergers in Einstein-Maxwell-dilaton (EMD) theory, varying the magnitude and relative sign of the input charges and the dilaton coupling $a$. In the weak-charge sequence, the dominant gravitational waveforms remain close to the neutral vacuum baseline of general relativity and are visually indistinguishable across the sampled couplings, consistent with earlier EMD simulations.
