Late-time OTOCs in Toda conformal field theory

Authors’ note: 74 pages, 10 figures

We study late-time out-of-time-order correlators (OTOCs) of semi-degenerate primary fields in two-dimensional $A_{N-1}$ Toda conformal field theory. In Liouville theory ($N=2$), we find that the OTOC decays as $t^{-3/2}$ when the continuum edge dominates, correcting a previously reported constant limit.

For $N=3$, a transverse contour pinch reduces the spectral integral to one dimension. Assuming that the reduced expansion is complete, in the continuous phase the regulated edge behaves as a discrete state, and the OTOC approaches a pure braiding phase.

As we check numerically, the edge block is a free-field correlator in a charge-neutral Weyl frame; this explains the pure phase and is consistent with crossing symmetry. For both $N=2$ and $N=3$, a dominant discrete residue gives a constant late-time limit.

Giwan Kim and Masahito Yamazaki · arXiv:2611.33744

Interfaces in Long Range CFT$_1$

We consider interfaces in the theory of a single free scalar in AdS$_2$ defined by adding relevant operators along portions of the boundary. We begin with Neumann/Dirichlet interfaces, computing the associated scaling dimension and $g$-factor ratio; we also explain how arbitrary interface correlators can be computed numerically efficiently.

Then we move on to interfaces between generalized free field and long-range interacting theories such as long range Ising, determining many quantities in perturbation theory.

Bastien Girault and Miguel F. Paulos · arXiv:2611.34705

Holography & Gauge/Gravity · hep-th

RMT${}_2$: Random Spectral Correlations for CFT${}_2$ and AdS${}_3$ Gravity

We present the full framework of RMT${}_2$, a consistent unification of random matrix theory and modular invariance. The systematic prescription, which augments the existing construction by incorporating spin, lifts random matrix correlators to $\text{SL}(2,\mathbb{Z})$ Poincaré sums of their Mellin transforms, in a manner which enforces random matrix correlations near extremality in every spinning primary sector.

The resulting RMT${}_2$ correlators are further expressible in terms of an orthogonal basis of Eisenstein series and Maass cusp forms.

Jan Boruch et al. · arXiv:2611.33291

Strings, Amplitudes & Supergravity · hep-th

Defect Correlators from Supergravity: Finding the Missing Contact Terms

We resolve a puzzle in the computation of holographic correlators using supergravity when defects are present. Previous direct supergravity calculations of defect two-point functions were found to differ from bootstrap results by a set of contact contributions.

We show that the discrepancy arises from two effects: nonlinear Kaluza-Klein field redefinitions in the bulk, which induce additional quadratic couplings when applied consistently to localized sources, and off-shell terms in mixed bulk--defect vertices, which can collapse internal defect propagators.

Qi Chen and Xinan Zhou · arXiv:2611.34082

Gravity & Cosmology

Bounds on Love Numbers and Quasinormal Modes from the Surface Impedance

The surface impedance of a body is fixed by its interior alone and encodes its entire tidal response. We derive bounds on Love numbers and quasinormal modes from constraints on the impedance that follow from either of two properties of a stable body: it is lossless, meaning that it dissipates no energy, or it is passive, meaning that it never gives back more energy than it has received.

The String Theory Photinoverse

Type-IIB string compactifications typically give rise to a significant number of RR-photons. These are generically massless and do not possess any light charged states, making them `superhidden'.

However, since supersymmetry is present at least at some high scale, corresponding `dark photinos' are also predicted.

The all-loop spectrum of GGK strings

We study the non-perturbative spectrum of the non-relativistic limit of string theory in AdS$_5\times$S$^5$, also known as GGK theory. In light-cone gauge, we show that the bosonic spectrum on a number of vacua is described at all-loops by free massive and massless scalar fields in AdS$_2$, confirming a conjecture by de Leeuw, Fontanella and Nieto-García, arXiv:2403.09563.

A Pole-Cut Formula for Holographic Extremal Correlators

We show that the holographic spectral function of a neutral massless scalar on the extremal Reissner-Nordstrom black brane in $AdS_5$ admits an elegant formula in terms of the quasi-normal modes (QNMs) and branch-cut data. In the extremal case, the spectral data comprises the zeros of the boundary source function, i.e. the QNMs, as well as its phase discontinuity across the branch cut on the negative imaginary axis of the complex frequency plane.

We show that, when $Λ> 0$, the conformal factor problem is absent in first order Einstein-Cartan gravity in which the spin connection and tetrad are independent off-shell.
