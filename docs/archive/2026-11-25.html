Flat Space Entanglement from Wedge Holography

Authors’ note: 39 pages plus appendices

We study holographic entanglement entropy in three-dimensional flat space by combining wedge holography with a controlled flat limit of AdS end-of-the-world branes. The limit takes a wedge of AdS$_4$ bounded by two AdS$_3$ branes to a Randall-Sundrum-like configuration of two flat branes, and the entanglement entropy of an interval in the two-dimensional corner CFT becomes that of an interval at null infinity of the branes.

The result has the functional form of the entanglement entropy of a two-dimensional Carrollian CFT, the proposed holographic dual of gravity in asymptotically flat three-dimensional spacetimes. However, the inferred central charges yield $c_M=0$ and $c_L\to\infty$ in the flat limit.

Adding a gravitational Chern-Simons term produces a $c_M$-like contribution, but we still find $c_L\to\infty$ and $c_M\to0$ in this limit.

Robert C. Myers and Bruno Sivilotti · arXiv:2611.31414

The Relaxation of Quantum Near-Extremal Black Holes. (I) Microcanonical States, Pruning of Quasinormal Modes, and the Aretakis Instability

The late-time relaxation of a near-extremal black hole is classically governed by an infinite tower of purely damped quasinormal modes. This poses a puzzle: the higher overtones decay at rates much larger than the energy $E$ of the black hole above extremality, so they would need black hole states below the extremal mass.

We resolve it by including the quantum fluctuations that become large when $E$ falls below a scale $E_b$.

Roberto Emparan et al. · arXiv:2611.31459

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Anomalies and the Berry Phase: From $π^0\toγγ$ to the geometry of the fermion vacuum

We review the chiral anomaly from a historical perspective, from the $π^0\toγγ$ puzzle and the work of Adler, Bell and Jackiw, to the Wess-Zumino-Witten (WZW) term. We then describe how the WZW term was motivated from quantum chromodynamics in 1983-85.

Integrating out the non-Abelian gauge fields, that have a gapped spectrum, gives a Nambu-Jona-Lasinio type theory of quarks, and integrating out the quarks, which are gapped by chiral symmetry breaking, gives the WZW model.

Spenta R. Wadia · arXiv:2611.33121

Inflation at strong mixing as a connection problem: effective field theory, Stokes phenomenon and the cosmological collider

We study a massive field strongly mixed with the curvature perturbation during inflation, in the regime where the latter is described at low energies by a single-field effective field theory (EFT) with a quadratic dispersion relation. What this EFT captures, and what lies beyond it, are encoded in a connection problem, which relates the solutions that define the vacuum at early times to those that describe observables at late times.

The Schwarzschild-Kerr crossover of high overtone quasinormal modes

A slowly rotating black hole is close to a nonrotating one, but its highly damped quasinormal spectrum is not. The Schwarzschild approximation holds only up to overtone numbers of order $χ^{-4/3}$, where $χ$ is the dimensionless spin.

We find that the frequency shift at this scale is a universal function, derived from the wave boundary conditions with an error estimate.

Yasuyuki Hatsuda · arXiv:2611.31394

Differential Equations for Feynman Integrals from Higher Residue Pairings

We develop a method for constructing differential equations for Feynman integrals directly from multivariate intersection numbers, without fibration. The large-$\varepsilon$ expansion of the intersection pairing, with $\varepsilon$ the dimensional regulator, is given by Saito's higher residue pairings, which localise at the critical points of a potential and admit closed multivariate formulae.
