Lead Story · hep-th

Conformal field theory from automorphic forms on a quantum group

We reformulate two-dimensional conformal field theory (2d CFT) in the language of noncommutative geometry. To this end, we introduce automorphic forms for the von Neumann algebraic quantum group $\mathrm{SL}^+_b(2,\mathbb{R})$.

A quantum measure space is a von Neumann algebra equipped with a weight. We study such spaces carrying a weight-preserving action of $\mathrm{SL}^+_b(2,\mathbb{R})$.

We argue that every unitary bosonic 2d CFT with central charge $c=1+6(b+b^{-1})^2>1$ is equivalent to a quantum measure space with an action of $\mathrm{SL}^+_b(2,\mathbb{R})\times\mathrm{SL}^+_b(2,\mathbb{R})$. In this dictionary, Virasoro primary operators are quantum automorphic forms and their scaling dimensions correspond to the spectrum of a quantum Laplace operator.

Dalimil Mazac · arXiv:2611.06907

Striped phases of N=4 SYM at finite density

We construct spatially modulated phases of strongly coupled $\mathcal N=4$ supersymmetric Yang--Mills theory at finite $R$-charge density. Compactification on a three-torus and unequal background charges isolate an unstable charge-density mode.

Its static threshold seeds a nonlinear branch of striped black branes in the STU model. The striped states have higher entropy than homogeneous states with the same energy and conserved charges.

Alex Buchel · arXiv:2611.06421

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Quasi-Normal Modes and thermalization in integrable Quantum Field Theories from the Thermodynamic Bethe Ansatz

We propose a Thermodynamic Bethe Ansatz (TBA) approach for describing thermalization in integrable quantum field theories. The exponential damping and oscillations when approaching thermal equilibrium can be understood as the analogs of quasi-normal mode (QNM) frequencies of black hole perturbations within the framework of holography.

Holographic intuitions and some general properties of thermal Green's functions motivate the existence of such a TBA formulation, as well as lead to the imposition of a nonstandard analytical structure on the QNM TBA, while comparison with integrable form-factor expansions fixes the remaining ingredients.

Zoltan Bajnok and Romuald A. Janik · arXiv:2611.06473

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

On phases of 4d $Spin/SO$ Yang-Mills theories

We study the low-temperature phase diagrams of $Spin(N)/SO(N)_\pm$ Yang-Mills theories as functions of the $θ$-parameter. In particular, for every even integer $N\geq 4$, we find a phase diagram consistent with the center/CP mixed anomaly, via the technique of mixed gauge theory.

The resulting phase diagrams are in perfect agreement with the phase diagrams of $Spin(N)/SO(N)_\pm$ super Yang-Mills theory softly broken by a small gaugino mass.

Ryo Hamachika and Takahiro Nishinaka · arXiv:2611.06058

Form Factors in AdS from the Worldsheet

We study AdS form factors describing closed strings scattering off D-branes. Assuming that these form factors admit worldsheet integral representations in terms of multiple polylogarithms, we determine their leading curvature corrections around the flat-space limit by matching their pole structure to CFT data.

Exact Splitting of Amplitudes and One-Loop Integrands: A Classical Origin of Zeros

We introduce a recursion based on hidden zeros that reconstructs complete tree level scattering amplitudes and one-loop integrands at generic kinematics. The recursion takes a universal exact-splitting form: both tree amplitudes and one-loop surface integrands are expressed as sums of products of complete tree functions, with the chosen zero conditions manifest in every term.

Anomaly effective actions for 4d $\mathcal{N}=1$ superconformal field theories

We present a comprehensive analysis of the Wess-Zumino anomaly effective action of four-dimensional $\mathcal{N}=1$ superconformal field theories in components. After reviewing the coset construction of the Wess-Zumino action in the presence of an unbroken subgroup, the gauging of the conformal algebra and the Wess-Zumino effective action for non-supersymmetric conformal field theories with a U(1) symmetry in two and four dimensions, we revisit the superconformal anomalies of $\mathcal{N}=1$ conformal supergravity.

The quasi-topological space of quasi-topological gravities

We investigate general properties of quasi-topological theories of gravity involving infinite towers of polynomial densities and of their regular vacuum black hole solutions. We show that every positive-mass regular vacuum solution necessarily possesses a de Sitter core, while Minkowski and anti-de Sitter cores are excluded.

The Start Time of Ringdown

We determine the convergence of quasi-normal modes sums for a point-particle plunging into a Schwarzschild black hole. While a dynamical amplitude model reproduces the waveform after the particle crosses the bounce radius, a constant amplitude sum converges only after the start time $t_S$, which corresponds to the advanced time at which the particle hits the central singularity.
