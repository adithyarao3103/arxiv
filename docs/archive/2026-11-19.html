Lead Story · hep-th

Learning the Anyon Atlas

Authors’ note: 24 pages, 11 figures

We extend our previous search for the modular data of topological quantum field theories in 2+1 dimensions from 22 to 42 anyons, identifying 150,679 new modular data. We do so by adding to our algorithm three rejection gates, two of them based on logistic regression, that discard representations of $SL(2,\mathbb{Z})$ unlikely to yield modular data.

We estimate that roughly 80% of the modular data between ranks 23 and 42 has been identified after closing under known constructions. With this data, we find counterexamples to a conjecture of Geiko and Moore that the reality of the higher Gauss sums suffices for time-reversal symmetry in Chern-Simons theories, and propose a refined criterion.

We demonstrate the scalability of this gated implementation by identifying prime modular data at rank 100 that is not realized by any Chern-Simons theory.

Jonah Berean-Dutcher et al. · arXiv:2611.24752

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

A Positive Metric on Four-Dimensional QFTs

A natural metric on the space of four-dimensional quantum field theories was introduced by Jack and Osborn in classic work on the local renormalization group. In perturbation theory, the Jack-Osborn metric determines the flow of the $a$-anomaly, and positivity implies the $a$-theorem.

We derive a non-perturbative generalization of the Jack-Osborn metric and show that it is non-negative by the averaged null energy condition (ANEC).

Thomas Hartman et al. · arXiv:2611.23370

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Computing $F$-Symbols from Modular Data

We present a strategy for computing the $F$-symbols of unitary modular categories with a self-dual simple generator, starting from their modular data, which almost entirely avoids the non-linear solution of the pentagon equations. The algorithm climbs a ladder of braid group representations on successively more strands of the generator until enough $F$-symbols have been determined to propagate them to a complete solution.

Jonah Berean-Dutcher et al. · arXiv:2611.24753

Grade Restriction Rule For D-Brane Transport In Non-Abelian Gauged Linear Sigma Models

We study the low energy behaviour of B-type D-branes in non-Abelian gauged linear sigma models. The main problem is to determine the grade restriction rule for D-brane transport from one phase to another along paths.

We analyze the convergence condition of the integral for the hemisphere partition function. We perform a thorough analysis in a particular model with two phases, one weakly coupled and one strongly coupled, both corresponding to Calabi-Yau threefolds.

Richard Eager et al. · arXiv:2611.24250

Gravity & Cosmology

Melvin-type Electromagnetized Kerr Black Hole in Kaluza-Klein and Einstein-Maxwell-dilaton-axion Theories

A naive application of solution-generating techniques to construct Melvin-type rotating black holes in the Kaluza-Klein (KK) theory in the presence of both electric and magnetic external fields generically introduces an undesirable Taub-swirling twist term. We investigate its origin and modify the electromagnetization procedure, thereby constructing an exact solution of the Melvin-type electromagnetized Kerr black hole in the KK theory without the swirling term.

Horizon kinematics and the fine structure induced by finite-energy Hawking radiation

Hawking radiation can modify the horizon structure of a black hole through the loss of mass and global charges. ER=EPR and IMP wormholes in quantum gravity

We analyze and refine the ER=EPR conjecture by studying IMP (Isenberg, Mazzeo and Pollack \cite{Isenberg:2001qe}) wormholes in QFT and quantum gravity, in the AdS/CFT context. IMP wormholes are solutions to the Einstein equations constructed from another general solution of Einstein's equations by gluing a handle on a Cauchy slice with a constant mean curvature, so that the initial data on the slice does not change in the regions away from the handle.

Only the disk: topological obstructions for warped AdS$_6\times S^2$ in Type IIB

Supersymmetric AdS$_6\times S^2$ solutions of Type IIB supergravity are warped products over a Riemann surface $Σ$. They are determined by two holomorphic differentials on $Σ$, with five-branes at poles on its boundary.

We derive restrictions on the genus and the number of boundary components of $Σ$.

We study the non-linear dynamics of Chern-Simons gravity with numerical relativity for the first time. We use the regularisation prescription to obtain a well-posed initial-value formulation in the gauges commonly used in numerical relativity, and we simulate head-on collisions and binary black hole mergers in the regime of validity of effective field theory, extracting the corresponding scalar and gravitational waves.
