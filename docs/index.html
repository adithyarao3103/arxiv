Lead Story · hep-th

Lifshitz Phase of Quantum Gravity: The Infrared Fixed Point of the (2+1)D Unimodular Hořava-Chern-Simons Model

Authors’ note: 5 pages; Mathematica notebooks included as ancillary files

We consider a nonrelativistic gravity theory in (2+1) dimensions with $w_\infty$ gauge symmetry that we call unimodular Hořava-Chern-Simons gravity. The theory has three dimensionless couplings, one of which is the Chern-Simons level $κ$.

At large $|κ|$, the theory flows from an asymptotically free UV fixed point to an IR fixed point with anisotropic scale invariance characterized by a dynamical critical exponent $z=2-\tfrac32|κ|^{-1}$. We speculate that unimodular Hořava-Chern-Simons gravity undergoes a phase transition from this scale-invariant phase to a confining phase when $κ$ drops below a critical value.

Maxim Zelenko and Dam Thanh Son · arXiv:2610.34024

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Exact lattice bosonization of the double-scaled $c=1$ matrix model

In a recent paper by two of us, it has been shown that entanglement entropy (EE) of matrix quantum mechanics (or, equivalently, of a non-relativistic fermion model) has a natural interpretation in terms of an exact bosonization of the fermion theory in terms of a lattice boson with lattice spacing $\sim 1/N$ (where $N$ is the rank of the matrix, equal to the number of fermions). The theory of this lattice boson is distinct from a latticized version of the "collective variable'' theory which is formulated in terms of the fermion density $ρ(λ)$ ("collective variable''); it is, in fact, more closely related to the Wigner distribution of the fermions.

Adwait Gaikwad et al. · arXiv:2610.34376

Holography & Gauge/Gravity · hep-th

Holographic entropy inequalities & typical states

As a consequence of the Ryu-Takayanagi formula, static semiclassical states in AdS quantum gravity share a set of special properties expressible as linear inequalities on their entanglement entropies. How general are these properties?

Do they still hold when we move away from the regime of validity of RT, i.e. static states in classical Einstein gravity? Do they perhaps hold even for some class of non-holographic states?

Guglielmo Grimaldi and Matthew Headrick · arXiv:2610.33905

Gravity & Cosmology · hep-th

On memory effects in odd spacetime dimensions

We establish the existence of a new electromagnetic memory effect in all odd spacetime dimensions. This effect is a universal property of electromagnetic fields sourced by charged particles of constant momenta, appears at the Coulombic order and is directly related to the Weinberg soft theorem.

We provide a unified derivation of this effect in all higher dimensions by evaluating the Maxwell constraint on a null plane at large, finite distance.

Ana-Maria Raclariu and Binsong Xiong · arXiv:2610.35283

Perturbative Holonomy Potentials and Vacua in Chiral Supersymmetric Gauge Theories

We establish global control of perturbative D-term data governing semiclassical vacuum sectors in chiral supersymmetric gauge theories on $\mathbb{R}^3\times S^1$. A closed-form, one-loop-exact D-term coefficient, including 4d Fayet--Iliopoulos (FI) parameters, gives a uniformly controlled leading potential away from massless thresholds and fixes local FI and Chern--Simons couplings near them.

We explore effective field theory (EFT) realizations of the dark dimension (DD) scenario in which the size of the DD is determined by the competition between a 5D quintessence potential and the Casimir energy. When only contributions from massless fields are considered, the Casimir energy does not have a typical energy scale, and the 4D dark energy (DE) scale is set by the height of the 5D quintessence potential.
