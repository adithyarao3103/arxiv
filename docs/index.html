Lead Story · hep-th

Expiscating the black hole singularity in holography

Authors’ note: 153 pages, 42 figures

We argue that the leading exponential rate in the high frequency asymptotics of holographic thermal two-point functions is independent of the conformal dimension and spin (held fixed) of the operator. We analyze the leading universal WKB spectral curve governing the asymptotics at non-vanishing momentum scaling with frequency.

We show that its classical action governs time domain signatures of the bouncing geodesic singularity, highly damped quasinormal modes, and the asymptotics of multi-stress tensor OPE coefficients. Beyond the leading order, we examine linear and dimension-dependent sublinear scalings of momentum with frequency.

In both regimes, we derive the connection matrix between the boundary and the horizon using exact-WKB techniques.

Hewei Frederic Jia and Mukund Rangamani · arXiv:2611.13261

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Categorical RMT

Random matrix theory (RMT) describes the level statistics of chaotic systems one symmetry sector at a time. We propose its analogue for local quantum systems on a circle with ordinary or non-invertible symmetries.

Symmetry defects define twisted Hilbert spaces, and the symmetry sectors are irreducible representations of the tube algebra, which acts within and between these spaces. Under our proposal, the symmetry fixes exact degeneracies and identifications among sectors, and ordinary RMT describes the rest, with unrelated blocks treated as statistically independent.

Chuanxin Cui et al. · arXiv:2611.13093

Strings, Amplitudes & Supergravity · hep-th

Rotational instability and quadrupolar order in BFSS matrix theory

We study the effect of angular momentum on thermal states of the BFSS matrix theory in its gravitational and high-temperature classical regimes. We focus on ensembles with only one non-zero angular momentum.

On the gravity side, the rotating ensemble is described by the singly-spinning D$0$ black hole. Beyond a critical rotation, we find that this solution becomes unstable to a non-axisymmetric quadrupolar perturbation.

Jun Liu and Jorge E. Santos · arXiv:2611.13122

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Giant Graviton Correlators: From Non-maximal to Dual, Bootstrap and Hidden Symmetry

We compute four-point functions of two light $\frac{1}{2}$-BPS operators and two heavy giant graviton operators in $\mathcal{N}=4$ super Yang--Mills theory at large $N$ and infinite 't Hooft coupling. Using bootstrap techniques, we determine the correlators for arbitrary light-operator dimensions and arbitrary allowed sizes of non-maximal sphere and dual giant gravitons.

We show that these correlators exhibit a hidden ten-dimensional conformal symmetry, allowing them to be organized into compact generating functions.

Qi Chen et al. · arXiv:2611.13444

Quantum Langlands correspondence for Painlevé II

We construct an irregular KZ-BPZ correspondence for quantum Painlevé II from a rank-three Wakimoto-Whittaker module. The irregular KZ equation and the quantum isomonodromy equation form a joint affine system whose Drinfeld-Sokolov reduction yields scalar BPZ equations.
