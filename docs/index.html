Lead Story · hep-th

Compact holography via polarized IKKT

Authors’ note: 60 pages + appendix, 11 figures

The polarized IKKT matrix model is a $U(N)$ supersymmetric matrix integral proposed to be dual to an $N$ D-instanton sector of Euclidean type IIB string theory. We show a direct path from matrix model saddles to supergravity saddles: matrix model resolvent $\to$ holomorphic function $\to$ 2D Coulomb potential $\to$ 4D Coulomb potential $\to$ supergravity fields, where the last step was previously described.

Here, the 2D electrostatics picture is an exact finite $N$ reformulation of a localization deformation of the matrix model that gives a constrained gas of dipoles in a background field. We show a matching between supergravity and matrix model on-shell actions and protected observables for all saddles.

We show that the supergravity saddles have a compact and non-singular metric in string frame with the Einstein frame asymptotic region replaced by a pointlike D-instanton source.

Arsam Najafian et al. · arXiv:2611.03807

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Chaos on the mass shell

We study particle production in high-energy states for theories given by an RG flow between an asymptotically free or conformal theory in the UV and a massive theory in the IR. Using the framework of detector matching, we argue that distributions of produced particles satisfy universal evolution equations with energy, determined by the spectrum of light-ray operators of the UV theory.

In particular, we interpret the UV Chew-Frautschi plot as a wave equation for particle densities on the mass shell, evolving with energy.

Alexandre Homrich et al. · arXiv:2611.03787

Holography & Gauge/Gravity · hep-th

From Z to all: cyclic sum rules for thermal CFT partition functions

The high-temperature expansion of twisted $S^{d-1}\times S^1$ partition functions of CFT$_d$, $d = 2κ$, has recently been shown to obey an infinite tower of universal constraints. We determine these constraints explicitly and demonstrate that they can be packaged into a single equation involving a large conformal diffeomorphism isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}_{κ+1}$, which cyclically permutes the thermal circle and the rotation angles.

Arnav Advant et al. · arXiv:2611.05281

Gravity & Cosmology · hep-th

Horizon exchange and the universal all-spin response of Kerr

The radial Teukolsky equation is invariant under exchange of the outer and inner horizons, although the physical boundary condition is not. We derive an exact monodromy relation between the corresponding Mano--Suzuki--Takasugi (MST) near-zone responses.

At leading order in frequency and to all orders in spin, this relation yields, for every multipole, an expression for the leading horizon-exchange-odd response. For gravitational perturbations, it determines the exchange-odd contribution to quadrupolar absorption and organizes the exchange-even dynamical response into a digamma function whose expansion accounts for the odd- and even-zeta structures found in Kerr-Compton calculations.

Pierre Vanhove · arXiv:2611.05054

Boundary $c\leq1$ Liouville CFT from the Bootstrap

We construct boundary Liouville conformal field theory at central charge $c\leq1$ with FZZT-type boundary conditions and show that it satisfies the Cardy--Lewellen sewing constraints on the disk and the annulus. Using the analytic bootstrap we derive the so far missing boundary three-point and bulk-to-boundary structure constants and a consistent disk one-point function as explicit meromorphic functions of the Liouville momenta.

An Exact QFT/Lattice Map for Fermions

We construct an exact map between continuum fermion fields and lattice fields in 1+1 dimensions. This map rewrites a continuum Hamiltonian as a lattice Hamiltonian with nearest-neighbor couplings and an infinite-dimensional local Hilbert space.

The resulting lattice model reproduces exactly the energy spectrum, global operator algebra, chiral symmetries, and anomalies of the continuum QFT on any finite lattice.

A Sojourn Through $c=1$ String Theory

We revisit the $S$-matrix, the thermal $S$-matrix and the thermal free energy of $c=1$ string theory from the worldsheet perspective. By numerically integrating Virasoro conformal blocks with DOZZ structure constants, we compute the scattering amplitudes and thermal amplitudes up to 6-point at tree level and up to 3-point at one loop, in addition to the genus two thermal free energy.

Bouncing and QNM convergence in Kerr

We show that the convergence region of the QNM representation of the retarded Green's function of the Kerr spacetime is set by complex-time bouncing singularities, as in our previous work for the Schwarzschild case. The main difference between Kerr and Schwarzschild is that there are two distinct QNM families, prolate and oblate, which have different convergence regions.

Gravitational-wave lensing beyond the diffraction integral: proper-time methods and the bound lens

Gravitational waves (GWs) can have wavelengths comparable to the Schwarzschild radius of their lenses, and detectors record their phase, so lensing requires wave optics. Its standard tool, the diffraction integral, follows from the particle path integral of a scalar wave on a static weak field, under the eikonal (one-way) and paraxial approximations, plus the thin-lens limit.
