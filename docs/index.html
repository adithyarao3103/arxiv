Lead Story · hep-th

99.9...% of the SU(2) BFSS ground state

We solve for the ground-state wavefunction of the SU(2) BFSS matrix model by applying the Rayleigh-Ritz method to an auxiliary operator $H_{\mathrm{aux}}$. We show that this operator agrees with the Hamiltonian on the ground-state subspace and has a spectral gap.

For SU(2) specifically, the representation theory of the singlet sector gives an astronomical reduction in the number of degrees of freedom. This allows us to construct an efficient variational basis whose inner products we compute exactly.

We demonstrate the method by computing a state that satisfies rigorous bounds on its energy $E\,g_{\mathrm{YM}}^{-2/3}\leq 4.18\times10^{-8}$ and ground-subspace overlap $\|P_{\ker H}ψ\|^2\geq 0.999997$. We compute ground-state moments with high precision, finding values consistent with the bounds from our matrix bootstrap.

Alexander Michel et al. · arXiv:2611.09837

Gravity & Cosmology · hep-th

Dressed AdS$_4$ black holes in M-theory from the 3d superconformal index

The superconformal index of 4d $\mathcal N\,{=}\,4$ SYM has been shown to exhibit a rich phase structure, whose holographic interpretation in AdS$_5$ involves (dual-)dressed black holes, gray galaxies, and graviton gas contributions. Here we extend the analysis to 3d CFTs, focusing on ABJM theory and the $\mathcal N=4$ ADHM theory.

We study the microcanonical superconformal index with two independent charges at finite $N$ and obtain exact signed state degeneracies up to large charges.

Evan Deddo et al. · arXiv:2611.08618

Holography & Gauge/Gravity · hep-th

A Song of Spice and Ice: A diagonal dual to dS$_3$ & its Galilean flat space limit

We propose a diagonal CFT dual to asymptotically de Sitter spacetimes in three dimensions (3d), constructed from the holomorphic part of the CFT dual at the future boundary and the antiholomorphic part of the CFT dual at the past boundary, in part inspired by an antipodal map between the two CFT copies. This gives equal and opposite imaginary central charges for the holomorphic and antiholomorphic sectors.

The corresponding diagonal holographic energy-momentum tensor expectation values are constructed from the Wavefunction at the future boundary and its conjugate.

Arjun Bagchi et al. · arXiv:2611.08600

Strings, Amplitudes & Supergravity · hep-th

Null Strings Gauged, Reloaded and Quantized, II: Null Strings on Flat Backgorund Have Discrete Spectrum

We continue our study of the quantization of null strings, strings with Carrollian worldsheets described by an action with two-dimensional (2d) diffeomorphisms, Weyl and Carroll-Weyl scaling as gauge symmetry. We perform light cone gauge quantization of the null strings on flat space background within Schrödinger representation, initiated in arXiv:2607.02970.

We show, within the regular separated Schrödinger sector, that bosonic null strings on a flat background have no critical dimensions, have discrete, nonnegative mass-squared levels and are hence free of tachyonic modes.

I. Rasulian et al. · arXiv:2611.09420

Field Theory & Symmetry

Analytic Bounds on Conformal Defect Spectra

Conformal defects describe impurities and boundaries at criticality. Their stability depends on the scaling dimensions of operators localized on the defects, but these dimensions are often difficult to determine.

We derive analytic upper bounds on the lowest nonvacuum scaling dimension using only two defect entropies and the dimension of a defect-changing operator.

Yuma Furuta et al. · arXiv:2611.08582

Discreetly Conformal: Higher-Order Chiral Scalars and the Discrete Series

With its constant shift symmetry gauged, a massless scalar on two-dimensional flat space defines a conformal field theory whose classical phase space is equivalent to that of a chiral and an anti-chiral Floreanini--Jackiw scalar. We show that this picture generalizes: After gauging a larger set of the shift symmetries of the massless scalar field, it decomposes into a pair of generalized chiral and anti-chiral scalars, described by actions with higher-order spatial derivatives.

Carrollian minimal massive gravity and the fate of third-way consistency

We construct the Carrollian limit of minimal massive gravity. The limit is singular for the algebra and regular for the Chern-Simons-like data.

The one divergent bracket points along the null direction of the contracted bilinear form. The Lagrangian is therefore finite while the brackets are not.

Existence of extremal solutions in the 1d conformal bootstrap

Extremal solutions and functionals play an important role in the conformal bootstrap. These are the solutions that saturate unitarity bounds, and the dual functionals that encode the optimal bounds.

While they have fundamental theoretical implications, their existence has not been rigorously established.

Quasinormal modes of Kerr--Newman--de Sitter black hole: Linear stability and strong cosmic censorship conjecture

We present an alternative formulation of coupled gravitational and electromagnetic perturbations of Kerr--Newman--de Sitter black holes, yielding decoupled radial and angular equations with a well-defined $Λ\rightarrow 0$ limit. We compute the quasinormal mode spectra using spectral and direct integration methods, finding excellent agreement.

Generalized Schwarzian thermodynamics from gravitational perturbations of near-extremal Kerr

We derive the generalized Schwarzian thermodynamics of near-extremal Kerr directly from four-dimensional gravitational perturbations of the near-horizon geometry. We construct two families of exact linear perturbations of the near-NHEK background: a non-normalizable mode that deforms the asymptotic boundary, and a normalizable mode supported near the horizon.

Particle production from noncommutative-inspired Reissner-Nordström black hole

By considering a massless Klein-Gordon field and the moving mirror model, we calculate particle production for a noncommutative-inspired Reissner-Nordström black hole sourced by smeared distributions of mass and charge. We show that noncommutative truncated trajectories diminish the emission spectrum at large frequencies, due to noncommutative effects on the external surface gravity of the black hole, in sharp contrast with the spectrum of a classical Reissner-Nordström black hole.
