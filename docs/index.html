Lead Story · hep-th

Non-singlets and the phase diagram of Matrix Quantum Mechanics

Authors’ note: 96 pages + references

The quantum mechanics of one $N\times N$ Hermitian matrix in the infinite $N$ limit is well known to define a two-dimensional string theory when the quartic coupling constant $g$ is tuned to a special negative value $g_*$. We study the thermodynamics of this Matrix Quantum Mechanics (MQM), treating the SU$(N)$ symmetry as a global symmetry.

Crucially, the non-singlets, i.e. states in non-trivial representations of SU$(N)$, implement the effects of vortices on the discretized worldsheets. This random lattice model has the low-temperature $c=1$ and high-temperature $c=0$ phases, with the Berezinskii-Kosterlitz-Thouless (BKT) transition separating them.

We map out the phase diagram in the coupling--temperature plane, which is separated by the critical stability curve $T_c(g)$ extending from $g_*< g <0$.

Igor R. Klebanov et al. · arXiv:2610.32856

Strings, Amplitudes & Supergravity · hep-th

Fermions Scattering Off Non-Abelian Rotors

We study the dynamics of $d=1+1$ chiral fermions interacting with a rotor impurity. The rotors are non-Abelian, taking values in either an $SU(N)$ group manifold, or an $SU(N)/SO(N)$ coset.

At low energies, the fermions pass through the impurity, seemingly unimpeded, but trailing a topological line that is necessary for current conservation. In certain cases, these topological lines exhibit non-Abelian fusion and braiding.

George Koundouros and David Tong · arXiv:2610.31780

Holography & Gauge/Gravity · hep-th

Shear viscosity from complex light-cone expansion

Near thermal equilibrium, the transverse momentum density in a fluid diffuses according to $ω(k) \sim - i {\cal D} k^2$ at small $k$, where the diffusion coefficient ${\cal D}$ is proportional to the shear viscosity. In field theories with a holographic dual description in terms of classical gravity, the shear-mode frequency $ω(k)$ approaches the light cone at large imaginary wave vector.

We develop a WKB approach to study this limit.

Inês Aniceto et al. · arXiv:2610.32892

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Defects in Topological Symmetric Orbifolds and their String Dual

We review defect topological field theories with finite gauge groups. We then concentrate on restriction and induction defects between symmetric orbifold theories and recall that the defects form an algebra.

This requires identifying the features of restriction and induction defects that are independent of the order of the symmetric group, which is natural in a grand canonical framework. The construction leads to Khovanov's categorification of the Heisenberg algebra.

Jan Troost · arXiv:2610.32654

Gauge conditions in the perturbative quantization of quadratic gravity and its quantum Lagrangian

In the perturbative formulation of the Hamiltonian of quadratic gravity, a specific gauge condition is required to hold the equivalence of the Hamiltonian equations of motion with the covariant field equations. Here we give robust evidence that this is an effect of perturbations, the exact nonperturbative equations of both formulations are equivalent, with no need of specific gauge conditions.

Jorge Bellorin et al. · arXiv:2610.31839

Discontinuity at the Pole

The exchange of a particle whose mass is taken to zero need not reproduce the exchange of a particle that is strictly massless from the outset. We derive a general on-shell diagnostic of the well-known discontinuity between the exchange of a massless particle and the massless limit of a massive-particle exchange, valid for a mediator of arbitrary integer spin S.

Melonic ladder holography with a single free bulk field in AdS$_{d+1}$

We derive a cylinder from the pair Casimir of a CFT$_d$: at zero centre momentum it depends only on relative dilations and rotations. Relative-coordinate radial quantisation then encodes exchanged family labels as cylinder energies.

For a melonic CFT$_d$ in the normalisable and stable range, the reduced ladder sum is the Robin boundary-to-boundary propagator of a single free scalar field in rigid AdS$_{d+1}$, with the cylinder as its conformal boundary.
