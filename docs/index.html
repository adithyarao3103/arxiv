Swing Surfaces in AdS/CFT

Authors’ note: 42 pages + appendices, 7 figures

We study holographic entanglement entropy in the flat limit of AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$, and clarify the relation between Ryu-Takayanagi surfaces and the swing surfaces proposed for flat-space holography. We show that the ordinary RT formula does not admit a well-defined flat-space limit and connect this to subtleties of the state-operator map in the flat limit.

We then construct an AdS$_3$ uplift of the swing prescription and develop its corresponding information-theoretic interpretation through two complementary approaches. First, we show that the swing surface length computes the mutual information between suitably chosen nearly null boundary intervals in a special class of pure CFT states.

The gravity interpretation of this result involves scattering processes which take place deep inside of the AdS$_3$ bulk.

Jacqueline Caminiti and Sabrina Pasterski · arXiv:2611.15854

Exact Infrared Triangle in Gravity with Long-Range Interactions

Einstein gravity coupled to massless matter exhibits universality in the infrared. In the presence of long-range interactions we show that a novel superrotation charge conservation is equivalent to the massless limit of the classical logarithmic soft graviton theorem, and we propose a formula for the corresponding tail to the displacement memory.

These results, which extend earlier work on gravity coupled to massive matter in a qualitatively different way, are exact in the gravitational coupling and reveal that long-range gravitational dressings resum.

Sangmin Choi et al. · arXiv:2611.15921

Gravity & Cosmology · hep-th

Cosmological Dressing Rules for Massive Scalars

The Schwinger-Keldysh formulation of de Sitter correlation functions comes in different guises including harmonic analysis and dressed flat space correlators. We observe that these are equivalent spectral representations of the same Sturm-Liouville problem.

Starting from the latter we derive dressing rules for scalar fields of arbitrary mass with auxiliary propagators in the form of Euler integrals that are accessible to flat space diagrammatic tools.

Jonathan Gräfe et al. · arXiv:2611.15870

Gravity & Cosmology · hep-th

De Sitter Gravity and Emergent Time from Fusion Matrix Product States and SYK

We propose a quantum-mechanical model dual to two-dimensional de Sitter (dS) gravity. It is a quantum channel, defined by a matrix product state that fuses one spin at each step, and is related to a limit of the Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev model without chord crossings.

Its simplest part is a random walk with an emergent $SL(2,\mathbb R)$ symmetry, which we identify with the symmetry of the future boundary of dS. It selects a time variable in which the walk obeys the Schwarzian action and yields the disk amplitude of dS Jackiw-Teitelboim gravity.

Shinsei Ryu and Yu-ki Suzuki · arXiv:2611.17045

Recent formulations of lattice chiral gauge theories define a complex fermion weight either through Craig's gauge-invariant overlap measure or, following Hsu and Witten, through a continuum Weyl determinant on a reconstructed gauge connection, with its phase fixed by the five-dimensional $η$-invariant.

Finite cyclic monodromy in BMS3 gravity

We investigate inducing data for three dimensional asymptotically flat BMS gravity on hyperbolic Virasoro orbits with winding, which contain no constant supermomentum, and the restriction of formal smooth equivariant sections to global Lorentz orbits. Using the developing map of the associated Hill operator we rederive their stabilizer and show that, in the spin double cover, it is the direct product of a line with a cyclic group of even order.

Holographic Counterterms from Restricted Weyl Symmetry in Even-Dimensional Einstein-AdS Gravity

We develop a symmetry-based approach to renormalization for even-dimensional Einstein-AdS gravity based on infinitesimal bulk Weyl transformations that preserve its field equations asymptotically. We determine a bulk-covariant completion of the Einstein-Hilbert density by requiring its variation under restricted Weyl transformations with parameter $Σ(X)$ to vanish modulo terms whose integrals remain finite as the radial cutoff is removed.

We propose that the large $N$ transseries of a holographic observable above the black hole threshold admits a precision bound, a limit on how precisely the transseries can determine the observable at finite $N$, set by the data of its D-brane instanton sectors.

Silent Heavy Fields: An Exclusion Rule for the Cosmological Collider

The cosmological collider uses oscillatory signatures in primordial correlators to probe heavy fields during inflation. This probe has blind spots, as heavy fields can keep ticking in time while remaining silent in the correlators.

In this letter, we derive an exclusion rule under which cosmological collider signals vanish.

The $(5,5)$ String

We study an $N=(2,1)$ heterotic string with right-movers on $\mathbb R^{2,2}$ and left-movers of signature $(5,5)$, obtained by adjoining the lattice $\mathrm{II}_{3,3}$. Unlike the standard $N=(2,1)$ strings it requires no null current, and its target space remains four-dimensional.

Justin Kaidi · arXiv:2611.17275

Twisting IKKT with branes

Motivated by the conjectural relationship between the IKKT matrix model and type IIB string theory, we classify supersymmetric twists of the matrix model, allowing for both dynamical and kinematical supercharges. Beyond the two twists available at any finite $N$, which we studied in our previous work, we find five additional classes at infinite rank which require non-commuting backgrounds associated with D-branes in IIB supergravity.

Self-Force and Bremsstrahlung in Finite Electrodynamics

We study the self-force and the accompanying Bremsstrahlung for a moving point charge in nonlinear electrodynamics with finite self-field and self-energy. The self-force in the Maxwell theory has been the subject of many studies, often by renowned physicists.

After reviewing Coleman's lucid treatise for Maxwell among them, we wish to explore the same question in the Born--Infeld theory, our prototype.

Cubic Curvature Deformations to the Kerr Black Hole Shadow

We investigate the effects of cubic curvature corrections on the shadows of rotating black holes within the effective field theory of gravity. Starting from a perturbatively corrected Kerr metric incorporating two independent cubic curvature couplings, we construct black hole shadows through numerical integration of null geodesics, without assuming separability of the geodesic equations.

A Universal Law for Horizon Formation in Black Hole Mergers

When two black holes merge, a common horizon appears around them as a single surface, which immediately splits into an outer and an inner one. We show that this event has a universal local form.

Without any symmetry assumption, the two surfaces separate as $(t-t_*)^{1/2}$ along a single, everywhere positive mode and share a drift linear in $t-t_*$.

Yu-Cun Xie and Vaishak Prasad · arXiv:2611.16168
