Lead Story · hep-th

Renormalization Group Flow from 4d Chern-Simons Theory

Authors’ note: 70 pages, 3 figures

The four-dimensional Chern-Simons theory coupled with surface defects is known to realize two-dimensional integrable quantum field theories (QFTs). We discuss the renormalization group (RG) flow of such two-dimensional integrable QFTs from first-principles Feynman diagram computations intrinsically in the four-dimensional framework.

We first derive the one-loop RG flow of two-dimensional QFTs realized by the four-dimensional Chern-Simons theory on $Σ\times C$ coupled with the order surface defects supported on $Σ$. Here, the affine Kac-Moody symmetry of the defects underlies the one-loop RG flow.

We then derive the one-loop RG flow of the principal chiral model (PCM) without Wess-Zumino term, which is realized using disorder surface defects, within the Batalin-Vilkovisky formalism.

Meer Ashwinkumar and Masahito Yamazaki · arXiv:2611.19517

Algebraic instantons and non-Gaussian vacuum in quantum mechanics

The standard instanton calculus in quantum mechanics is based on harmonic minima in the potential. When the perturbative vacuum is anharmonic, $V\simeqλx^{2n}$ with $n\ge2$, the standard construction fails.

We study this problem for a particle on a circle and subject to a potential $V(x)=16λ\sin^4(x/2)$. The instanton and its fluctuation operator are known in closed form, but they differ qualitatively from the familiar ones.

Rahim Aziz et al. · arXiv:2611.19761

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

On reflection positivity of lattice chiral fermions

We point out that the overlap Weyl fermion, the spacetime lattice realization of a Weyl fermion underlying Lüscher's construction of lattice chiral gauge theories, is not reflection positive in two and four dimensions at finite lattice spacing. Consequently, the standard Osterwalder--Schrader reconstruction does not yield a Hilbert space with a self-adjoint Hamiltonian.

In two dimensions, we further show that the overlap Weyl fermion cannot be cut open: the lattice action becomes nonlocal in the presence of a boundary, as expected from the gravitational anomaly of the continuum field theory.

Zhiyao Lu and Shu-Heng Shao · arXiv:2611.20084

Gravitational Memory Correlators and BMS Symmetry

We compute the n-point memory and mixed energy-memory correlators for scalar decay into gravitons using the in-in formalism at leading order in perturbative quantum gravity. We obtain compact results for arbitrary n and explicitly verify the supertranslation flux balance relation between memory, energy, and Bondi mass detectors, realizing asymptotic symmetry constraints on infrared-finite observables.
