Lead Story · hep-th

Geometric Quantization of the Giant Graviton Master Moduli Space

Authors’ note: 37+21 pages

We describe a remarkable reorganization of the space of gauge invariant operators of free or twisted $U(N)$ gauge theories as combinations of single-trace and determinant operators modulo certain "open loop equations". The reorganization has a transparent holographic interpretation, describing how states at finite $N$ can be reproduced by imposing certain $N$-independent gauge constraints on the combination of the closed strings Fock space and a geometric quantization of the moduli space of Giant Graviton D-branes, with quantization parameter $N^{-1}$.

The moduli space of Giant Graviton D-branes is reproduced from tachyon condensation of auxiliary D-brane/anti-D-brane pairs which are "Koszul dual" to the D-brane system which supports the original gauge theory.

Kasia Budzik and Davide Gaiotto · arXiv:2611.18539

Strings, Amplitudes & Supergravity · hep-th

What Comes Out Of Monopole-Fermion Scattering?

We describe what happens when chiral fermions scatter off magnetic monopoles. It has long been known that, in many situations, the outgoing state cannot be constructed from any finite number of excitations.

In the language of 2d CFT, it lives in a twisted sector of the Hilbert space. Here we explain what this means from the perspective of a d=3+1 observer.

We show that an ingoing one-particle excitation is scattered into an outgoing state surrounded by a soft cloud which consists of an infinite number of low energy particle-anti-particle pairs.

David Tong · arXiv:2611.17416

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Microcausality Bounds on Dispersion Laws

Microcausality holds in any state, including those that spontaneously break Lorentz invariance, and requires the poles (and other singularities) of the retarded Green's function to satisfy $\mathrm{Im}\,ω\le |\mathrm{Im}\,k|$ throughout the complex plane. Building on the work of Heller, Serantes, Spaliński and Withers, we study its consequences for dispersion relations that are linear at small $k$, like Goldstone bosons, assuming tree-level, non-dissipative dynamics.

Paolo Creminelli et al. · arXiv:2611.17407

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Link invariants in nilpotent group gauge theories

We consider 3-dimensional topological gauge theories with nilpotent gauge groups, both finite, that is Dijkgraaf-Witten theories, and continuous, that is Chern-Simons theories. Abelian topological quantum field theories are exactly those which do not detect the Borromean rings, and nilpotent gauge groups provide the natural next steps beyond the abelian case.

We show that the untwisted Dijkgraaf-Witten theory with gauge group $G$ detects Brunnian links with up to $n+1$ components, but no Brunnian links with more components, if and only if $G$ is $n$-step nilpotent.

Thomas Nicosanti et al. · arXiv:2611.18237

Holography & Gauge/Gravity

Superstars, quantum wormholes and von Neumann algebras

We entangle heavy 1/2-BPS states in two copies of $\mathcal{N}=4$ SU($N$) Super-Yang-Mills (SYM) theory. Each copy is separately dual to an extremal, supersymmetric, singular black hole geometry, in which the horizon coincides with a singularity.

General ideas in the AdS/CFT correspondence suggest that the entanglement between the two copies should lead to some form of connectivity between the two geometries---a kind of wormhole.

Vijay Balasubramanian et al. · arXiv:2611.17404

Mirror symmetry of $\mathcal{N}=(0,4)$ boundary conditions with Nahm poles

We propose mirror symmetry between $\mathcal{N}=(0,4)$ boundary conditions with Nahm poles and Neumann boundary conditions with boundary Fermi multiplets in 3d $\mathcal{N}=4$ non-Abelian gauge theories, including $U(N)$ SQCD, $T[SU(N)]$ and the ADHM theories.

Tadashi Okazaki · arXiv:2611.18118

Graded unitarity constraints on simply-laced affine Kac--Moody algebras

We investigate constraints imposed by four-dimensional unitarity (recently formalised as graded unitarity) on possible simply-laced affine Kac--Moody vertex algebras arising from four dimensions via the SCFT/VOA correspondence. Under the assumption that the $\mathfrak{R}$-filtration is weight-based with respect to the currents and the Casimir operators, with appropriately shifted $R$-charge assignments for the latter, we demonstrate that all values of the level other than a discrete set are incompatible with four-dimensional unitarity.

Supersymmetric indices provide stringent tests of dualities between superconformal field theories (SCFTs). We establish infinite families of Schur-index identities among four-dimensional $\mathcal N=2$ Argyres-Douglas (AD) theories, with the logarithms of their fugacities related linearly.

Black Hole Probes under T-duality

Attractor black holes are finite-size probes of infinite-distance limits in the vector-multiplet moduli space of type IIA Calabi-Yau compactifications. We ask whether the hypermultiplet moduli space admits analogous probes.

T-dualizing BPS black holes along a Euclidean time circle and along a spatial circle, which realizes the temporal and spatial c-map, we construct the dual type IIB D-instantons and axionic strings, on $T^6/(\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2)$ and on a generic Calabi-Yau threefold.

We study two-point functions of $\frac{1}{2}$-BPS local operators in the presence of a $\frac{1}{2}$-BPS surface defect in six-dimensional $(2,0)$ theory, dual to an M2-brane in $\mathrm{AdS}_7\times$S$^4$.

Modular Structure of the ADHM Effective Twisted Superpotential

The Cardy limit of M2-brane indices retains nonperturbative information through the effective twisted superpotential, including corrections exponentially suppressed at large rank. We investigate the flavor dependence of these corrections in the three-dimensional $\mathcal N=4$ $\mathrm U(N)$ ADHM theory at the universal twist.

From pure spinor modules to superspace superfields

We relate the pure spinor formalism to the superspace description of constrained superfields and gauge multiplets, mainly for abelian two-dimensional N=(2,2) supersymmetry, with three- and four-dimensional N=1 examples for comparison. For each multiplet we give an explicit module over the supertranslation Chevalley-Eilenberg algebra and match its reconstruction with the known superfields.
