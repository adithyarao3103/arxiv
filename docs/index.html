Constraining the spectral gap of 2d CFTs just above c=1

Authors’ note: 7 pages, 9 pages of supplementary material, 13 figures, 2 tables, code available at https://github.com/Kevin-Langhoff/cft-gap

We numerically study the space of compact unitary two-dimensional conformal field theories (2d CFTs) with central charge $c>1$ using the modular bootstrap. We focus on bounding the spectral gap $Δ_{\rm gap}$ of such theories, namely the smallest scaling dimension among the non-vacuum primary operators.

We use both a primal method that directly searches for consistent spectra and a dual method that yields rigorous exclusions. A key feature of our analysis is requiring the degeneracies of primary operators to be positive integers.

At $c$ very near $1$, the integrality constraint greatly reduces the allowed spectral gap in both methods. For example, at $c-1 = 3\times 10^{-3}$ integrality strengthens the rigorous bound from $Δ_{\rm gap} \lesssim 0.5$ to $Δ_{\rm gap} < 0.041$.

Nathan Benjamin et al. · arXiv:2611.07011

Multipartite Entanglement, TQFT Partition Functions and PL Bordism

In a previous work we conjectured that the multi-invariants of the ground state of a gapped $(d-1)+1$-dimensional system, labeled by bipartite $(d+1)$-edge-colored graphs encoding closed oriented $d$-manifolds $M$, compute the partition functions $Z(M)$ of the low energy topological quantum field theory (TQFT), up to non-universal factors determined by the local structure of the triangulation. In this paper we determine which combinations of partition functions can be isolated from the non-universal factors.

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Conformal Bootstrap Unblocked

We develop a novel conformal bootstrap formulated directly in terms of conformal symmetry, reflection positivity, and crossing symmetry, without explicitly relying on the operator product expansion or conformal blocks. By constructing Gram matrices from states created by pairs of local operators and imposing spectral constraints through conformal generators, we obtain a hierarchy of semidefinite programs that directly constrain correlation functions.

Li-Yuan Chiang and David Poland · arXiv:2611.07004

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Defect Anomalies at a Distance

Surface defects carry Weyl and gravitational anomalies that generalize the central charges of two-dimensional conformal field theories (CFTs) and characterize the anomalous response of gapless surface modes to background geometry. These anomalies usually appear as contact terms on the defect.

We explain how they are encoded at a distance, in scheme-independent integrated bulk stress-tensor correlators. We derive sum rules for them in terms of defect operator data, establish a lower bound on the boundary Weyl anomaly in three-dimensional CFTs, and illustrate our results with explicit examples.

Oleksandr Diatlyk et al. · arXiv:2611.08476

Background Effects on Thermal Free Energy in Conformal Field Theories

We compute background corrections to the thermal free energy of free conformal field theories (CFTs) with a global abelian symmetry defined on a box with all sides infinitely long by turning on a flat gauge field in general dimensions. This result is applied to the U(N) maximally supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory, the ABJ(M) theory, and the free U(N) vector model in four, three, and two spacetime dimensions, respectively.
