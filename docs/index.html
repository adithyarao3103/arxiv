Lead Story · hep-th

Relativistic Hydrodynamics as an Intersection Problem

Authors’ note: 9 pages

Ideal relativistic hydrodynamics admits a covariant description as an intersection problem in a symplectic space associated with spacetime. The independent fields are differential forms of degrees 0,1,3,4.

Spacetime diffeomorphisms and shifts of the momentum one-form act Hamiltonianly; the zero locus of their moment map is a coisotropic subvariety encoding the transport equations. The spacetime metric and equation of state determine a Lagrangian submanifold, whose intersection with the coisotropic subvariety is precisely the Lichnerowicz--Carter system.

We explain the geometric meaning of the canonical flow parameter, derive circulation and Ertel invariants directly from the constraints, and identify energy--momentum conservation with covariance of the constitutive Lagrangian.

Nikita Nekrasov and Paul Wiegmann · arXiv:2611.28735

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

Wormholes do not contribute to supersymmetric partition functions

We show that wormholes connecting multiple asymptotic boundaries always spontaneously break at least some of the symmetries preserved by their boundary conditions. Their configurations then form nontrivial orbits of the broken symmetry, and for the case of a fermionic symmetry, the contribution of each orbit vanishes.

We conclude that wormholes do not contribute to moments of supersymmetric partition functions. This extends results on supersymmetric indices by Iliesiu, Kologlu, and Turiaci.

Jan Boruch and Douglas Stanford · arXiv:2611.28210

Strings, Amplitudes & Supergravity · hep-th

Bring Your Own Instanton: Monopole Catalysis from Sourced SU(2) Instantons

We show that Rubakov-Callan catalysis of proton decay in the field of a 't Hooft-Polyakov monopole proceeds through "sourced SU(2) instantons", which are novel topological gauge configurations sourced by fermions and carrying unit instanton number. Unlike vacuum BPST instantons, these sourced instantons do not give rise to exponentially suppressed rates.

We relate the winding number to the number of fermionic zero modes, suggesting an underlying index theorem for sourced instantons.

Csaba Csáki et al. · arXiv:2611.28208

Field Theory & Symmetry · hep-th

An all-orders proof of gradient flow for multiscalar $φ^4$ theory in four dimensions

We prove that the renormalisation group flow of multiscalar $φ^4$ theory in four dimensions is a gradient flow to all orders in perturbation theory. The proof uses the Weyl consistency conditions of Osborn and Jack, and it assumes two properties of perturbation theory: that the generating functional with local couplings is finite (also assumed by Osborn and Jack), and that the known expansion of massive Feynman integrals at large momenta holds with a bound on its remainder at each momentum.

Marten Reehorst · arXiv:2611.28206

Field Theory & Symmetry

Gradient formula from the sphere for perturbative RG flows near critical dimensions

We study the gradient property of perturbative renormalisation group flows in $d=d_c-\varepsilon$, with a potential and a metric regular at $\varepsilon=0$ up to logarithms of gauge couplings. We state properties of the renormalised functional with local couplings on the sphere $S^d$: finiteness, a local Weyl anomaly, the Ward identity of a conformal Killing vector, rotation properties and statements on the free theory.

Analytic $k$-essence Inflation

In this work we develop a fully analytic $k$-essence inflation solution which is compatible with the observational constraints on inflation, without the need for the slow-roll assumption being imposed dynamically in the field equations. The analytic solution is obtained for a kinetic scalar sector of the form $K(X)= \frac{X^n}{X_s^{n-1}}$ and for the scalar field satisfying a tracking condition $\dotφ^2=γH^{-m}$.
